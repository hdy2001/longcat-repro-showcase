The Meteor Difference

What Makes The Meteor, The Meteor

❖

Film, Actual Film

One of the last houses in the county still projecting 35mm and 70mm. The scratches, the grain, the faint flicker — all of it is part of the picture.

✦

The 1927 Auditorium

Two hundred and eighty-eight seats beneath a hand-poured plasterwork sky. Every seat was measured for the perfect sightline — to the screen, and to the stars riding the projector's beam.

✺

Curated Double Bills

No algorithms here. Our programmers pair films the way sommeliers pair wine — thematically, passionately, and occasionally controversially.

☄

The Comet Curtain

The original 1927 velour curtain parts like a falling meteor before every single show — as it has, without fail, for ninety-nine years.

☾

Craft Concessions

Real butter, smoked salt, espresso pulled on the same machine since 1962, and a small amber ale brewed two blocks away.

◈

The Pre-Show & The Dark

Vintage cartoons and trailer reels before every screening. Then: phones off, ushers with light wands, and two hours where the outside world simply stops existing.
